Concept design

Built on a shape Egypt has trusted for 4,500 years.

Our greenhouse concept takes the geometry of our roots and puts it to work in the desert. A pyramid module for showcase and research sits beside long production houses with hipped ridge roofs: the same pyramid form, stretched to give growers more floor space.

How the shape works.

  • 1
    Light from every sideFour sloped glass faces catch the low morning and evening sun, not just the midday light.
  • 2
    Air that moves itselfWarm air rises into the apex and vents out, drawing cooler air in at plant level.
  • 3
    Strength with less materialTriangular faces brace each other and shed wind and sand rather than catching them.

Good produce starts long before the harvest.

Being a direct operator means we answer for the whole journey. These are the principles we hold ourselves to on our farms and with the growers we work with.

Care for the soil

Farming our land so it stays productive for the next generation, not just the next season.

Respect for water

Irrigating to what each crop needs, because in our region water is the scarcest input of all.

Shorter supply chains

Fewer hands between farm and buyer means fresher produce, fewer miles and clear accountability for quality.

Less waste

Planning harvests around confirmed orders and keeping the cold chain unbroken so more of what we grow reaches a table.
