Privacy policy.

How we handle the details you share with us through this website. Last updated 2 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Agrinova Global General Trading L.L.C., a fresh produce company at the Fresh Market, Ras Al Khor, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Agrinova. If you have any question about your information, email info@agrinova.ae.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through our forms:

  • Enquiry form: your name, company, email address, the type of business you are, and your message.
  • Supplier registration: your company and farm details, location, products, volumes, seasons, certifications, facilities, and your contact name, role, email and phone number.
  • Customer registration: your company details, location, products of interest, volumes, order and delivery preferences, and your contact name, role, email and phone number.

Please don't send sensitive personal information, such as health or financial details, through the forms. We don't need it.

We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of our own on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk to you about produce, supply, pricing and delivery.
  • To review your registration and, if we agree to work together, set up and manage your account as a supplier or customer.
  • To keep business records as UAE law requires.

We use your details because you asked us to contact you and agreed to it when you submitted the form, and because they are needed to take steps towards a business relationship with you. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it to send you marketing you didn't ask for.

Who else sees it

Your details are used only by Agrinova. We don't sell, rent or share them with other companies for their own use. We use trusted service providers to run this website and our email, and they handle your details only on our behalf. We may also share information if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

If we don't go on to work together, we delete enquiry and registration details within 24 months of our last contact. If you become a supplier or customer, we keep your details for as long as we work together and afterwards for as long as UAE law requires for business records.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw your agreement to be contacted. Email info@agrinova.ae and we'll respond within 30 days. These rights follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
